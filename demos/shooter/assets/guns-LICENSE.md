# Gun models

`game/src/assets/guns.bin` holds models of the "Ultimate Gun Pack" by
Quaternius (July 2019).

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-guns-pack
  (`ultimate_gun_pack_by_quaternius.zip`)
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Attribution is not necessary.
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models in the game: every gun of the pack except the six sniper rifles,
and the `Bayonet_2` accessory as the knife. The repository keeps only the
converted file. To convert again, download the pack and run
`scripts/convert-gun.ts` on its `OBJ` folder.
